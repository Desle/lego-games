# Play-test visuals: the alien ship's beam, the broken barrels, the bricks' trails

Three bugs the player found in the Classic port. Each is a case of the render trace (`play-test-visuals.trace.spec.ts`; run it with `pnpm --filter @lego-games/lego-racers-web exec vitest run --config vitest.trace.config.ts play-test-visuals`, about 90 seconds). A request can name a `watched` drawing (the original's triangles by the texture they match, the port's by `instance/material`) and a route-driven scenario (`isRouteDriven`: the golden trace's closed-loop drive with the use key pressed from `powerUp.useFrame`).

## What the original decides about drawing an instance

`Race_Render` (`0x004354d0`) draws a world with `0x00416040` (its lists of instances) and every instance first asks its visibility (`0x100240b0`, vtable slot `+0x14` of both the skeleton instance `0x10056fc8` and the mesh instance):

- the **sphere** (`0x100286d0` reads it, computing it with slot `+0x4c` when its radius is below 0): centre and radius of the instance. An **animated** skeleton instance (flag `0x10000`) takes them from the **clip** it plays (`0x10023ef0`: clip keyword `0x31`, four floats, centre then radius, in model units), scaled by the model's scale times the instance's and carried by the instance's frame; while two clips blend, the mean of both centres with the larger radius plus the mean's distance from the second centre. The bones do not move it. Port: `getSkeletonInstanceSphere`, clip `boundingSphere`;
- the squared distance from the eye to that centre against the **level distances** (`+0x84`, `+0x88`, `+0x8c`): the first level whose distance is not below it; none left (a negative distance, or 0) hides the instance. Hazards and events write these distances: the gate (`0x34`, parts to 0), the rock (`0x2e`, `-1`), the sphinx, and the 53 events below;
- the frustum test of the sphere (`0x1002bc20`: left out when the centre is farther than the radius behind a plane).

The viewer ignored all three for the race's moving instances: it showed level 0 of every instance of the three worlds all the time, hidden by the simulation or not. Now `showHazards` hands the simulation's distances and spheres to `SceneObject.setInstanceRuntime`, and `selectLevels(camera, frustum)` applies them. An instance the simulation neither hides, nor culls by a sphere, nor gives several levels is left as it was (the mesh instances' spheres, from the mesh, are not ported).

## 1. The alien ship's beam (RACEC2R3, Alien Rally Asteroid)

The beam is the skeleton instance `beamdumy` (`ufobeam`, material `arabeam`, 16 triangles; `BLENDED.WDB`) standing where the ship `ufofly1` stands, playing the same clip. The capture hazard (`0x48`, `0x0048dd20`) never touches it. `EVENT.EVB` has one **53 event** for it (identifier 3, keyword `0x41` + `0x33`: `beamdumy` of the second world).

53 events (`0x00461070` reader, array `+0x84`, `0x00463700` set-up, `0x004637c0` begin, `0x00463800` end; the docs said "switches three values of an object, not ported"): the three values are the object's **level distances**. Set-up saves them in the event (`+0x24..+0x2c`) and, unless the block is braced (`0x3c`, event `+0x30`), writes `-1.0` (`0x004b1c1c`) to the object, so it is **hidden as the race loads**. Begin writes the saved distances when the block is not braced and `-1` when it is; end the other way round. The beam is therefore drawn while event 3 runs (the port: frames 201 to 318 and every 304 frames after; signal 3 comes from the events' timers), and not in the introduction. The port drew it all the time (`extra_in_port beamdumy/arabeam x16` on frames 5 to 130 of the idle scenario). RACEC0R3's six `mmlaser1..6` (identifiers 60 to 65) are the only other 53 events.

Port: `Event53` handlers (`track-logic`, `setObjectLevelDistances`), `createTrackLogic` (the set-up, before the hazards load as `Events_Load` is before `Hazards_Load`), `getInstanceLevelDistances` and `getWorldWithInstanceLevelDistances` (`world-animation`, mesh instances looked up before skeleton instances as the original's three lists are). **Verified:** the beam's triangles are absent in the original and the port on frames 5, 50, 100 and 130. **Not verified:** the beam's pose while it is shown (no recorded frame has the camera looking at it while event 3 runs; the idle scenario's camera does not, the introduction is before the window). The sphere culling is in place for it (the clip sphere is 407.7 model units around the ship).

## 2. Broken barrels and other pieces left standing

The shortcut barrels of RACEC0R1 are four skeleton instances (`block`, `barrel`, `barrel01`, `barrel02`) that the barrier hazard (`0x34`, `0x0048ef70`) sets to level distances `[0, 0, 0]` when signal 7 opens it ([surface-signals.md](surface-signals.md)); the rock (`0x2e`) swaps `mmrocof` and `mmrocon` with `-1`. Nothing applied those writes: the port kept the pieces where their clip leaves them. `RACEC0R1, barrier` (the golden trace's drive, a cannon ball at frame 1460; the original opens the barrier at 1477): the original draws 24 triangles of `barrel2` on frame 1470 and none on 1478, 1490 and 1550; the port drew 98 (`block` 20, three barrels 26 each) on every one of them. Fixed by the instance runtime above. **Not verified** (not reached by any traced scenario): the rock swap (RACEC0R3 frame 2477, a shielded racer's touch), the sphinx's `blowup`, the door of RACEC2R3.

## 3. The sparkle around the power-up bricks

A power-up brick (`0x68` bytes, `0x00457640`, vtable `0x004b1710`) holds two instances of the POWERUP world: `+0x34` the brick (`gen`: `pubricky`) and `+0x38` its trail (`genblen`: `putraily`, 14 triangles, the quad flips with its clip, additive `source_alpha`/`one` blend, vertex alpha fading). Slot `+0x10` (`0x00457900`, from `PowerUps_Draw` `0x0045a7b0`) draws the brick with the material `pbrickP/S/T/M` (`+0x54`) and slot `+0x14` (`0x00457930`, from `PowerUps_DrawBlended` `0x0045a8a0`, called by the race's per-frame draw `0x00435a00`) draws the trail with `ptrailP/S/T/M` (`+0x58`, kinds 1, 2, 3, 4 as `0x0045bae0`), both at the brick's position and scale (`0x00453b30`, `0x00453b90`: position, instance scale `+0x58` = brick `+0x30`, then the instance's draw) and in states 0, 2 and 3. The turn of the brick is its clip `pubricky` (23 frames, 0.03 a millisecond, looping), not the power-up system's spin angle (`+0x199c`), which the port used; the trail's clip has 11 frames. Both clips are advanced by the power-up world's update (`0x00416090` at system `+0x60`, `0x0045a490`) with the frame's whole time step.

The port drew only the brick, turning by `brickSpinAngle`. Now `PowerUpWorld.brickClockMilliseconds` (the sum of the steps) drives both clips (`getEffectFigureState`), a trail pool follows the brick pool, and each instance (brick and trail, power-up and enhancement bricks alike) is left out when the sphere of its clip, scaled by the model's scale times the brick's and put at the brick's position, is beyond its level distance (160,000 squared) or outside the frustum (`getIsBrickInstanceDrawn`: before, the bricks were culled by their position and not by the frustum, and the enhancement bricks' trail, which the original draws only where its sphere is inside, had looked as if it was never drawn). `RACEC2R3` frames 5, 100, 170 and 250: the original's 100 unmatched trail triangles (`pupred2`, `pupblue2`, `pupyell2`) are all matched, no brick or trail triangle is extra (the frame's triangle count equals the original's at 250: 2108), and the bricks' bodies, which differed by the spin, now match too (what is left is the known `isAlphaTestEnabled` device state). The barrier case (`RACEC0R1`, white `pupwhit2` enhancement trails missing in frames 1500 to 1550 before) matches as well.
