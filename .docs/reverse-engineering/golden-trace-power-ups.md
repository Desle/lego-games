# Golden trace: power-ups, bricks and projectiles

Companion of [golden-trace.md](golden-trace.md). `power-ups.golden.spec.ts` grants the player (and, with `POWER_UP_COMPUTERS=1`, every racer) a brick of each kind 1 to 4 at each enhancement level 0 to 3 at frame 162, presses the use key at frame 165 and prints the first divergences per scenario:

```
POWER_UP_KINDS=1,4 POWER_UP_LEVELS=0 POWER_UP_REPORT=/tmp/p.txt \
  pnpm --filter @lego-games/lego-racers exec vitest run --config vitest.golden-trace.config.ts power-ups.golden
```

`POWER_UP_DUMP=<dir>` writes the scenarios as JSON (for probes). The grant writes racer `+0xccc` and `+0xd58` in the original (`golden_trace.py`, `powerUpGrants`) and the held brick and level in the port.

Fields (`power_up_trace.py`, `power-up-trace.ts`): `powerUps.bricks` (state `+0x3c`, `+0x40`, timer `+0x4c`, flags `+0x50`, kinds `+0x5c`, `+0x60`, `+0x64`), `powerUps.enhancementBricks`, `powerUps.effects` (active list head first: pool, state, timer, level), `powerUps.explosions`, `racerN.powerUps.level`.

## The warp's bounds quirk (ported)

The golden run draws every frame, so the draw callbacks change the sim. What the original does, and what the port does for it (`racer/types/vehicle-bounds`, `race/controller/warp-draw`):

- `0x00440160(vehicle, scale)` writes `-1.0` into the radius (`+0x10`) of the vehicle's four parts (`+0x3c` car model, `+0x40`, `+0x44` wheels figure = the display frame, `+0x48` driver) and the scale into `+0x58`; the sphere getters (`0x00411c60`, `0x00411ca0`) refresh a part with a negative radius first, with the radius and the centre scaled by `+0x58` (`0x0040e0b0`, `0x004113d0`). The raw centre stays as it was. Port: `getRacerWithPartsMarked` (`Vehicle.boundsCaches`, `Vehicle.bodyScale`, `Figure.scale`, and the display radius the ground probe reads through its getter).
- The warp's first half (`0x0045dbe0`, every racer's warp, after the frame's update): the fade scale is the table value at `(-ftol(time * 0.002 * pi * -162.97466) - 0x100) & 0x3ff` (a sine over a second), and while less than 250 ms remain the parts take it with a negative radius. The transition (`0x0045dc90` state 2, the `racer_warp_started` event) sets the scale back to 1.0 and marks again.
- The carried half (`0x0045d940`, only for a local player's camera): the display figure goes to the origin facing -y (slots `0x08`, `0x40`; position writes mark the radius, rotation writes do not), `0x0043e620` puts the car model, the second figure and the driver on that frame (the driver at the seat), the parts draw (which refreshes them), and the display figure goes back (its radius `-1` again, its centre left at the origin). The car model's rotation (`vehicle.bodyRotation`, the next lean's input) and the driver position stay at the origin frame until the next update. Port: `getRacerAfterWarpDraw`. When the camera is number 3 the parts are not drawn (no refresh); the sim does not know the camera number, so that case is open.
- `Vehicle_Update` (`0x0043e070`) returns after the driver update and the composite recompute when racer flag `0x10` is set (the emitters do not run), which `updateVehicleEmitters` already does. Every update refreshes all four parts (`boundsCaches` back to null).
- The warp start's vehicle part (`0x0043dcd0`, `0x0043dbb0`, the flags `0x14` cleared, `0x0043fd70`, `0x0043fdb0`) is `stopVehicleEffectsForWarp`; the landing (`0x0043db60`, the reset) sets the vehicle flags 2 and 4. The skid marks (vehicle `+0x1e4`) and the parts' tint (`+0x84`) are presentation and not kept.

## Computers' warp and the launcher variant (ported)

- Scripted start `0x00429a90` and end `0x00429af0` (`startCarBodyWarp`, `endCarBodyWarp`): two-point flags `0x30008`, speed factor 4.5, height target 6 at rate 3; the end restores the default speed factor, height target 0 at rate 15 and clears the flags. A computer is not carried (`0x0045d780` needs `+0xdc`, which `0x0045dc90` sets only for a human); its body flies the route itself. At the landing `0x0045e080` puts its display frame 5 above the ground under it, which the next scripted pass reads as its previous position.
- The fade (`0x0045dbe0`, called for every active effect by `0x0045a8a0`) runs only in the frames where a local player's racer is on the track (`Race_Render` draws the other frames with `0x0045a7b0`, which has no fade); `0x0045d940` runs for the camera racer in the other frames.
- The reset of a warp effect (`0x0045e000`) calls `0x00440160(1.0)`. When a new warp takes the effect over (`0x0045b2e0`, in the racers' power-up pass after the frame's vehicle update) a racer that is off the track keeps its parts marked until the next update; one on the track has them read again at once.
- The launcher variant (`0x0045d560` with the third argument, set by the warp pad `0x0048af80` through power-up system `+0x1998`): the timer is 0, `+0xc0..` take the destination and `+0xcc..` the facing, `+0xd8` is 1; `0x0045dc90` state 2 stores the racer's position (`+0xb4`), `0x0045d780` interpolates from it to the destination by `timer / 1500` (the z subtracts from the destination's x: `+0xc0` is read where `+0xc8` belongs) for any owner, and the landing starts from the destination with the launcher's facing. Ported from the decompilation; no golden scenario touches a warp pad, so it is not verified against the original.
- The parts' `+0x84` values are the car shadow's draw distance squared (`0x0043fa50` draws a decal when the camera is nearer): 250000 normally, -1 (never) from the warp's start (`0x0043dbb0`) until the landing, and 250000 again in the warp's own draw (`0x0043db60`). The shadow, like the oil slick and the scorch marks, is a decal projected onto the world's triangles (`0x00414a30`, `0x00414c90`); the shadow is ported (see [decal.md](decal.md)); the vehicle's skid marks (`+0x1e4`) are particle emitters and not drawn yet.

## Computers grid: magnet, curse and wand cells (fixed)

- Magnet `0x0043a130` / `0x0043a1a0`: a human's driver gets flag `0x80` and its vehicle starts the tire smoke (`0x0043dc00`) while held; a scripted body's target speed factors (`+0x7e8`, and `+0x7ec` unless car body flag `0x40000`) go to 0 and back to 1; `0x004395a0` ends a tumble.
- Curse (4/3): a warp or curse taken over by a racer's use (`0x00439100`, in the racers' pass after the vehicle update) ends its registry entry in the same frame, so the registry is synced again at the end of the frame.
- Wand (1/2): `0x00454d70` hands the rest of the step, after the countdown, to the jitter, the hold and the sparks.
- Still open in the grid: kind 1 level 3 (rocket: the missile's shape centre).

## Open

- Shape centres: the hook's bounds centre is placed with a hypothesis (the frame faces along the horizontal velocity); the barrel's spin and the missile's animated figure bounds (its first registered centre is about 6 units off the owner) are not ported.
- Warp: the route (checkpoints), the carry (`0x0045d780`), the start (`0x004395d0`: flag 0x10, timed effect and spin end, tows end, flag 0x1000) and the landing (`0x0045dc90`) are ported for a human's free body. The bounds quirk, the vehicle's smoke and fade state, the computers' scripted warp and the launcher destination are ported (see the warp sections). Open: the oil slick and scorch decal callers and the skid emitters (vehicle `+0x1e4`).
- Debris animation lengths are still the empty values of `EMPTY_PROJECTILE_MODEL_DATA` (the warp route now comes from the checkpoints); `trackTargets` (the TGB points the cannon and grapple aim at) are empty.
- Sound-driven random draws (`tauntNearestRacer`, `updateRacerSounds`) are stand-ins: a draw is missing or extra after some hits (wand, frame 199).
- `racec0r3` shows a track event difference at frame 797 (`track.events.2a[61]`) that the random cursor used to hide.
- Brick colours per kind belong to the render-trace branch (PR 37): `pbrickP`, `pbrickS`, `pbrickT`, `pbrickM` (`0x00458a80`, `0x0045ba90`).
