# Hazards, emitters, the world animation and the sky in `Race_Update`

Source: `LEGORacers.exe` (English, 1999-07-22), decompiled and, for float code and the vtables, disassembled or read as bytes. Ports: `packages/lego-racers/src/modules/hazard` (the classes), `emitter` (the pools), `world-animation` (new), `sky` and `track-logic` (the session that runs them). The files they read are in [hazard.md](hazard.md), [emitter.md](emitter.md), [sky.md](sky.md), [animation.md](animation.md), [material-animation.md](material-animation.md) and [skeletal-animation.md](skeletal-animation.md). Anything not proven is marked **hypothesis**.

## Order in `Race_Update` (`0x00435220`)

World animation `0x00416090` (track `+0x390`, local `+0x3a4`, blended `+0x398`), power-ups, tagged positions, triggers (environment then main), `Emitters_Update` for `+0x2150` then `+0x248c`, `0x00493a20`, `Events_Update`, **`Hazards_Update`**, `Timers_Update`, `0x00494f60`; the racers' sub-steps; `0x00492870`, **`0x0048ae00`** (hazards that follow the cameras), **`Sky_Update`**. The port runs the worlds, triggers, emitters, events, hazards and timers in one track-logic session (`updateTrackLogicFrame`), the hazards' camera update after the sub-steps and the sky last (`updateRace`). Loading: `Events_Load`, then `Hazards_Load`, then `Timers_Load`; the only load draws are the 2A events' and the three of hazard `0x2d` (its loader calls its own Begin).

## The world animation (`0x00416090`)

For a world: every material animation entry with flag 2 runs `0x004104c0`; every mesh instance `Instance_ApplyVelocity` (`0x10027f40`; velocity zero, nothing moves); every skeleton instance `0x10023b10` (clip time, then the position moves by the velocity: x and y add stored products, z the unstored one); the world's cameras `0x004047b0` (the camera lock; the world's sprites are not updated here, they are only drawn: [world-sprite.md](world-sprite.md)).

- **Material animation entry** (24 bytes): flag 2 plays, flag 4 loops, `+0x10` the time. Playing: `time = store(ms · rate + time)`; a non-looping entry whose truncated time reaches its length stops (flags 6 clear) and rewinds; otherwise the table row is the last of rows `first … first + count − 1` whose time is not past `trunc(time) mod length` (`0x00410560` does the same for the emitters' particles with `trunc(rate · age)`). `GolDP.dll` `0x1002e640` (`0x10025da0`) starts every entry an instance refers to, **looping**, when the world links, so those entries run from the start.
- **Skeleton instance**: flags `+0x5c`: `0x10000` animated, `0x20000` blending, `0x40000` looping, `0x80000` finished, `0x100000` continue, `0x400000` second clip loops, `0x2000000` wrapped. Linking starts the clip of keyword `0x35` (`0x10023a70`) and sets `0x10000` (cleared when there is none). `0x0040dae0` (start a clip at a time) keeps `0x10000`, sets `0x40000` and clears `0x20000`, `0x80000`, `0x100000`, `0x400000`. `0x0040e360`: finished when flag `0x80000` is set or the clip does not loop and its time is at its last frame. Level distances `+0x84..+0x8c` are the squared distances a level shows up to; a negative value hides the instance (`-1.0` at `0x004b4548`).

## Emitters (`Emitters_Update` `0x00489fa0`)

Two pools: `race +0x2150` (10 emitters, `EMITTER.EMB`, material animation 0 of the global world) and `+0x248c` (4 emitters, the race's own `.EMB`, material animation 0 of the local world; this is the pool hazards and 3D events use). An emitter object is `0x18c` bytes: definition `+0`, handle back-reference `+4`, the particle system `+8` (16 particles of `0x38` bytes), velocity offset `+0x148`, position `+0x154`, basis `+0x160`, time since the last birth `+0x184`, age `+0x188`; 100 handle slots (8 bytes) at pool `+0x14`.

## The hazards

`Hazards_Update` (`0x0048add0`) calls each object's vtable `+0x14`; the bus (`StartSignal` `0x00461ef0` and the others, see [track-logic.md](track-logic.md)) ends by walking the hazards: `0x0048aad0` (start), `0x0048abb0` (start and stop), `0x0048ab20` (stop), `0x0048ab70` (force end: lifecycle 3), `0x0048ac30`, `0x0048ad10`, `0x0048aca0` (racer triggers; only class `0x49` answers vtable `+0x20` with 1). A hazard whose identifier (`+8`, `-1` never) equals the signal begins when idle (`0x0048a470`) and ends when not (`0x0048a490`); then vtable `+0x28` sees the start signal and its position. Lifecycle `+0xc`: 1 idle, 2 active, 3 asked to end (the base update `0x0048a460` calls End). The port keeps a `HazardInstance` (definition, identifier, lifecycle, class data), runs the walks in `hazard-runtime.ts` and stores the result at each index after the handler returns (a re-entrant change to the same hazard is overwritten, as in the track logic).

## The final race's teleporter (RACEC3R0), found with a real session

The shortcut is a door (`door`, skeleton instance at (-148, 250, 17)) with a collision world instance `doorcol` (the barrier cars hit), the `shootme` material on the door face (cars pass, projectiles hit: a shot starts signal 1, which opens the door through the 4B and 28 events) and, behind it, the `warptrig` material (`0x10009`: cars pass, hit event with identifier 0) on a plane in the doorway; a car crossing it fires the warp pad hazard (`0x49`, identifier 0) through the surface hit (`0x00462580` with `0x00462ae0`).

Two port bugs, both fixed from the original:

- **The invisible barrier.** Event `0x52` (vtable `0x004b1c3c`; begin `0x00463990`, end `0x004639b0`, update `0x004639d0`) is not a no-op: while it is not inactive (a racer inside the trigger `1001` that tags `doorcol`) every update puts the collision instance `doorcol` on bone `keyword54Integer` of the skeleton instance `door`: position = the bone's own translation (bone slot `+0x40`) times model scale times instance scale, carried by the instance's frame; forward and up = rows 0 and 2 of the bone's rotation (bone slots `+0x30`, `+0x38`, GolDP `0x10002f50`, `0x10002f90`) turned only by the instance's rotation, then the instance's orientation builder (slot `+0x40`, `0x00410900` in the exe). The port left `doorcol` at its closed place for ever, so the opened door still blocked the car. Checked: with the door closed the computed frame equals the loaded one bit for bit; open, the door lifts 37 units. The per-frame update is ported from the disassembly and not compared in the emulator (**hypothesis** for the open pose).
- **The pad never warped.** `hazard.power_up_activated` (warp) was applied only for effects of the track logic frame, but the pad begins inside the racers' physics (the surface hit), so its launcher warp was dropped. It is now applied where every track effect is applied (`getRacerSetAfterTrackEffect`), as the original calls `0x0045b260` inside `0x0048af80`.

`final-race-teleporter.spec.ts` pins both: the champion car is driven by pure pursuit along the checkpoints and through the opened door (signal 1 started as the shot does) until it crosses `warptrig`, and must warp to the pad's destination; without event `0x52` following the bone the car never gets through. The session that found it could not be kept as a replay: it was recorded with the old pause menu (see [pause-menu.md](pause-menu.md)), so replaying it now drives a different race.

## What the hazards do to racers

Through the collision registry. A hazard registers in its Begin (`CollisionRegistry_Register` `0x0042fb50`, entry `{owner, type 4, flags 1, 0, 0, shape = hazard + 0x10}`: `0x0043d1b0` puts a type 4 entry on the list `+0x48` that `0x0043d270` walks for every racer, along the x extents of the racer's volume) and ends the entry in its End (`+0x24 = 0`). The shape is a `0x00411a40` sphere: the loader sets its radius (`0x00410f10`), the update its centre (`0x00411cc0`, which also rewrites the x extents). The vtable's slot `+0` is the touch: lava `0x0048dcd0`, ghost `0x0048ce90`, capture `0x0048e050`, rock `0x0048e660`, crane `0x00490490`; the hammer's is `0x004513d0` (a no-op: it is a type 3 body, see below).

Port: each class keeps its sphere in its data and answers `getRegisteredSphere` (the sphere while the lifecycle is active or asked to end, else none); `race` keeps a `hazard` registry object per registered hazard (registered at once after the track logic frame and again after the racers, like the power-up objects), `getRegistrySphere` reads the sphere, `answerRegistryContacts` calls `session.touchHazard`, and the `hazard.racer_affected` effects (`spun`, `flung`, `captured`, `released`) are applied to the racer by `getRacerAfterHazardReaction`. A racer with a vehicle object (`+0xb2c`) would take the other branch of the capture; the port has none (as for the magnet). Verified: `racec0r3` of the track logic golden trace (the rock's touch at frame 797 opened the collision object `mmrocc` one step apart) now matches the original for 1,500 frames. The touch callbacks `0x0048dcd0` (lava), `0x0048ce90` (ghost), `0x00490490` (crane) and `0x0048e660` (rock) have differential specs (600 cases in both precisions: the car body slots they call through the racer's vtable, with their arguments, and the bus calls, recorded by patched code, against the port's effects). Not differential-tested: the capture's touch `0x0048e050` and the update positions (`0x0048da10`, `0x0048cbb0`, `0x0048dfa0`, `0x00490190`), which need skeleton instance objects in the emulator; they have unit tests only. The golden trace's demonstrations never drive a racer into a lava monster, ghost, capture or crane (a census of 6,000 frames of every track found no such touch), so the racer physics after those touches are not compared with the original yet. Classes in the races: hammer `RACEC0R0`, `RACEC2R2`; crane `RACEC0R1`; lava and rock `RACEC0R3`; ghost `RACEC1R0`; capture `RACEC2R3`; warp pad `RACEC3R0` (already a racer trigger).

**The hammer is not a touch.** Its Begin (`0x0048be00`) registers a type 3 entry, and `0x0043d1b0` hands that to `0x0043aa40`, which puts it on the racers' collision list `+0x2c` (the list the racer pairs sweep, `0x0043ab90`), not on the touch list `+0x48`. Its vtable slot `+0` is `0x004513d0`, a no-op. The sweep pairs it with a racer like two racers: spheres along x, then the box test `0x00441330`; the racers' listener `0x00438560` (type 3) then runs for the racer with the hammer as the other shape. That shape has no racer (`+0xe0` is 0), so the function applies the impulse to the racer only (car body slot `0x20` with the direction and the impulse), plays the hit (`0x004220e0`) and returns before any racer-against-racer branch. The hammer's shape (`hazard + 0x10`, constructor `0x004411d0`, mass `0x469c4000` = 20,000 and the three dimensions of the file through `0x00441210`) is a rigid body whose centre of mass (`+0x30`) and orientation `0x0048be70` rewrites every update: it follows the chain of bone 1 up to the root (each bone's transform; for names starting `rk` the bones' frame rows 0 and 2 become the box's axes through the instance frame), scales the point by the model scale times the instance's, and moves the figure at `+0xf4` (radius half the largest dimension). **Ported** (golden trace `royal-knights-hammer`, bit for bit, hammer fields included). The loader `0x0048bbf0` gives the figure (`+0xf4`) a radius of half the largest of the three file dimensions and the rigid body (`+0x10`, constructor `0x004411d0`, `0x00441210`) the file's box, scale 1, mass 20,000 (inverse `1 / 20000`) and **no velocity: nothing integrates it** (the old hypothesis that the velocity follows the centre was wrong). Begin creates the node with flags 1 (not 5): the sweep `0x0043ab90` calls the hammer's own listener (`0x004513d0`, nothing) and then, because flag 4 is clear, the racer's with the pair swapped (`0x0043ab10`), so the racer is always the first shape whichever comes first in the list; two bodies do nothing. `0x0043d270` skips a node whose shape has `+0xe0` = 0. `0x0048be70`: after the common update it takes bone 1's chain (point through each bone's transform, `x scale`, instance slot `+0x2c`) for the centre of mass and the figure's position; for a name starting `rk` the **bone 1's** rows 0 and 2 (bone slot `+0x20`) are turned by the rotation of each bone of the chain from bone 1 up (bone slot `+0xc`) and by the instance's rotation (slot `+0x34`) and become the figure's axes (`0x00410900`). The bones hold the pose made when the instance was last **drawn**, so the update reads the clip of the previous frame (`posedClip`) and, once the instance is out of the camera's view, the original stops following (the hammer froze at frame 697 of the golden trace, 50 frames after the car passed): the port keeps following. `0x00438560` with a second shape that has no racer (`+0xe0` = 0) moves the first car out, applies the impulse to it alone (the second body's velocity 0, inverse mass and centre in the denominator), plays the hit (`0x004220e0`) and returns before the contact flag, sounds, sparks, attack and timed effects. Port: `HAMMER.getCollisionBody`, `RacerSet.collisionBodies` and `collisionOrder` node numbers (`racerCount + index`), `getRacerSetWithCollisionBodies` (race, before pass 4), `answerOverlap` and `respondToBodyContact` (racer). Also fixed on the way: `getInstanceRotation` (`0x10026b50`, `0x1002f530`) rounded like doubles; it now rounds like the x87 (instance rotations match the original bit for bit). **Open**: the pose when the hammer's instance is not drawn (camera visibility); the hammer starts only for a human racer (trigger 53 skips computers), the golden trace drives a human the lap backwards (`getLapDrivingFrames`, which steers the right way: `getRouteDrivingFrames` steers away from its aim, which is why that scenario gets stuck at (61, 669)); the hammer is never hit by the computers in the traces.

## Finding: the crane's sphere follows the last drawn pose (not ported)

Replaying a real session (`RACEC0R1`, frame 1209) the port spun a computer racer at a crane that the original did not touch. The original's registered sphere (hazard `+0x14`, radius `+0x20`) stayed at one fixed centre while the port's followed the crane's clip: the crane's update (`0x00490190`) reads the bone chain of the skeleton instance without posing it (vtable `+0x58`, `+0x18`, `+0x4`), whereas the 52 event (`0x004639d0`) and the hammer pose first (`+0x5c`, `0x10023580`). The bones are posed by the draw, so the crane's sphere is the pose of the last frame the instance was drawn in, which depends on the camera. The port poses the instance from its clip at every read. **Hypothesis:** exactness here needs the cameras' visibility in the simulation; not ported (a multiplayer server has no camera either).

## Sky (`Sky_Update` `0x0041ccb0`)

`advanceSky` (the settings' clocks and the transition) and the background world's animation `0x00416090`, once per `Race_Update` with the unscaled-by-substeps frame time `dt`, at the end of the frame. `RaceState.sky` holds the `SkyState` and the background `WorldAnimation`.

## Viewer

The race mode drives the track world's skeleton instances from `RaceState.trackLogic.worlds.track` (so the hammer swings with the simulation's clip, not the viewer's clock) and draws the live particles of both emitter pools as points (`race-particles`). The particles' sprite materials are not drawn.

## Open

Skeleton poses of hazard instances that are not ported (`0x0048be70` the hammer, `0x0048c680`, `0x0048e7f0`), the hazards' sounds, launcher-owned power-up effects, the camera hooks of `0x2f` and `0x32` (the port passes no camera positions), `Hazard_Reset` (vtable `+0x24`), mesh instance scrolling and sprites, and the vehicles' and power-ups' own spawns into the common emitter pool.
