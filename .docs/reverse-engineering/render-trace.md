# Render trace: what the original draws, against the viewer

The golden trace compares the simulation. The render trace compares what the player **sees**: every triangle the original hands to Direct3D, against the triangles of the viewer's three.js objects. `pnpm run render-trace` runs seven races (several tracks, a mirrored second pass) and four front-end screens in the emulator, and the port beside them (about 2 minutes). It skips cleanly without the virtualenv or the original files and is not part of `pnpm run check` (the specs are named `*.trace.spec.ts`; the viewer package's `vitest.trace.config.ts` runs them).

## How the original draws

`GolDP.dll` transforms on the CPU. The game draws through a Gol device object (`0x5041a208` in the emulator) whose slots `+0xc8764` (transform variant) and `+0xc8768` (cull and draw) `FUN_10012f50` fills in:

- the transform (`0x1000be20`, `0x1000c160`, `0x1000c630`, `0x1000caf0`, `0x1000cd20`, `0x1000bfb0`, `0x1000c2d0`) multiplies each vertex by **one 4x4 matrix** (`[device +0xc8518]`, row vectors; built per instance by `FUN_10008910`/`FUN_10008a50` as instance frame times camera) and divides by w: `x = x'/w`, `y = y'/w`, `1/w`. Three variants (`0x1000c630`, `0x1000caf0`, `0x1000cd20`) hold a clip-style matrix and apply the viewport scale and offset (`+0xc8400`) after the divide; the recorder folds that into the matrix;
- the draw (`0x1000ebd0`, `0x1000edf0`) drops a triangle whose screen turn is not negative (`(c.x-b.x)(a.y-b.y) - (a.x-b.x)(c.y-b.y) < 0` keeps it: counter-clockwise on screen) and calls `DrawIndexedPrimitive` (`0x4`, FVF `0x1c4` = XYZRHW, diffuse, specular, one texture coordinate set) with the remaining triangles in the order `c, b, a`;
- there is no `SetTransform`; the viewport (`SetViewport2`) is 640 x 480.

## The recorder

`reverse-engineering/emulator/render_trace.py` fakes the Direct3D 3 device: it keeps the render and texture-stage states, hashes the pixels of the surface bound by `SetTexture` (sent once per surface), and records every `DrawPrimitive` and `DrawIndexedPrimitive` (vertices as given, indices, the state, the texture hash, the model-to-screen matrix, the transform variant) per frame. `presentation_trace.py` runs the real race loop with scripted time and keys; `front_end_trace.py` runs `GameApplication_RunFrontEnd` (it needs `qsort`, stubbed in `front_end_stubs.py`) with keys tapped per frame.

## What is compared

For every recorded frame, the port's three.js objects (`getPortWorldTriangles`: the track, the cars, the effects, the sky; world positions, the texture, the render state, the colours) are projected with the **original's** model-to-screen matrix of the track (the candidate that matches best, `getCandidateMatrices`), culled with the original's rule (a mirrored object's faces are turned inside out first, as three.js does), and matched triangle by triangle on the screen (`compareRenderFrame`): corner positions (0.25 px), texture coordinates (0.001), diffuse colour (2 levels), state (texture operation, blending, alpha test, depth) and texture. A texture is identified by its pixels: the original's 16-bit surface is compared with every texture of the race at a 16 x 16 grid (colours within the 5/6-bit quantisation), so a triangle drawn with the wrong picture shows as `texture`. Triangles the original clipped against the viewport (corners exactly on its edge) are left out on both sides. The race HUD's images and the front end's rectangles are compared as rectangles (`compareQuadPlacements`).

With the original's camera substituted, a match proves the geometry, the UVs, the textures and the placement of every object; the **camera itself** (field of view 60 degrees, `race-viewport.ts`) is not compared: use the clip matrices in the recorded frames.

## Results

On the first frames of seven races 90 to 99 percent of the original's triangles have a partner in the port (the specs require 85).

The bug reports checked: "some races misaligned versus the rendered map" is finding 1 (the viewer's track was never mirrored); "power-up colours wrong" is finding 2; "driver figure textures misplaced" is partly finding 3 (shadowing by name) and finding 8; **"faces upside down" and the bat helmet** are not reproduced: the six champion figures of every traced race match in UVs and textures, and a built driver (the helmet parts) is not reachable in the emulator, whose front end does not run the car and figure builders. Trace gap: drive the builder screens to compare built figures.

## Adding a scenario

Add a `TraceScenarioRequest` to `RENDER_CASES` in `render-trace.trace.spec.ts` (the frames to record go in `recordFrames`; the race idles through the introduction and the countdown unless `isSteering`). A front-end case is a list of tapped scan codes in `front-end-render-trace.trace.spec.ts`.
