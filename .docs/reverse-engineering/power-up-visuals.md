# Power-up visuals: what the original draws, against the port

`pnpm run render-trace` has sixteen power-up cases (`Royal Knights, power-up <kind> <brick>/<level>`: cannon `1`, shield `2`, turbo `3` and oil `4`, each at levels 0 to 3; RACEC0R0; the player is granted the brick at frame 300 and presses use at 305; frames 307 to 385). Run one with `pnpm --filter @lego-games/lego-racers-web exec vitest run --config vitest.trace.config.ts render-trace -t "power-up shield 2/0"` (`RENDER_TRACE_FRAMES=312` records one frame, `RENDER_TRACE_GROUPS=<file>` writes the mismatch groups).

The library the models come from is `GAMEDATA\COMMON\POWERUP.WDB`: its mesh and skeleton instances are what the exe looks up by name (`FUN_004119a0`): `Explsn` (mesh `hedra01`), `barrel`, `spikexp`, `warpprt` (mesh `warphole`), `magnet`, `TurboL0..2`, `turb0f1..2f2`, `dmissil`, `shield0..3`, `shldin0..3`, `curse`, `cgreen`, `magring`, `insd`, bricks. Keyword `0x3f` of an instance is the texture scroll per second (`warpprt` 3, 2; `shield0..3` and the first flame 0, 4; the second flame 0, 2). Textures and materials come from the libraries `DTURBO0..2`, `POWERUP`, `GRAPPLE`, `WARPHOLE`, `CURSE`, `BARREL`, `CGREEN`.

## Ported

| Effect              | Original                                               | What it draws                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Shield              | `0x0045bd30` (start), `0x0045c160` (draw)              | Two figures of `shield0..3` (`sheout0..3`, one skeleton and animation set, clip 0 looping). Frame: the display figure's position 2 above, first row = its first row, third row = world up made perpendicular (`0x00410900`). The figure is double-sided (the engine draws the back faces, device slot `0xa4`, then the front faces). The texture scrolls (0, 4) per second. The inner shields `shldin*` are looked up and never used. |
| Rocket              | `0x00456db0`, `0x004568f0`, `0x00456ce0`               | The figure `+0x128` plays clip k of `DMISSILE.ADB` while it launches (frame: the owner's display frame), then stays on that clip's last frame; its frame is the motion's `frameRotation` and position. The flame (`aftrburn`) is part of the animated pose.                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Cannon ball         | `0x00451a50`, `0x00451f30`                             | A sprite of material `cannon` (texture `cannon16`), 5 by 5, camera-facing, centred on the ball. Sprites are drawn like particles (`GolDP.dll 0x10009a70`, `0x10014e50`), with the diagonal bottom left to top right.                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Turbo               | `0x0045c8f0`, `0x0045cf90`, `0x0045d150`, `0x0045d200` | Three figures per level (`TurboL*`, `turb*f1`, `turb*f2`): 2 behind and 3 above the display position, first row = the display figure's second row (side), third row = its third row. Clip 1 in the active state, clip 2 while ending. The flames are drawn with alpha 0 until the last 50 ms of the starting state (not drawn here).                                                                                                  |
| Warp portal         | `0x0045d560`, `0x0045dbe0`                             | In the first phase (state 2, 500 ms) the figure `warpprt` (mesh `warphole`) sits on the racer, scaled by the fade table (`getWarpFadeScale`), its texture scrolling (3, 2) per second.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Camera after a warp | `0x0045d940`, `0x0045dc90`                             | Both call `0x0043a3e0`, the camera's snap request: the port snaps the chase camera on the frame after a warp draw, so the driver is not seen from the wrong side after the drop.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |

Hypotheses: the turbo figures' animation leads the effect timer by one frame (33 ms) and their texture scroll lags it by 71 ms (measured against the trace); the warp portal keeps the racer's current rotation (the original copies it at the start).

## Not ported (open)

- **Tether (`rope`) and wand beam (`lightng`).** Both are strip figures: meshes of rows of three vertices (u 0, 0.5, 1, v = row) whose bones the grapple and the wand place each frame (`0x00494850`, `0x00494ca0`, bone callback `0x0040d670`); 154 and 258 triangles in the cases. Needs the strip mesh builder and the bone placement.
- **Effect emitters are never positioned** (`cannsmk` `0x00451b50`, `0x00454360`, `dynsprk`, `lghthit`, `oilbub`, `trbsmke` `0x0045ce20`): their particles stay at the origin of the common pool, beyond the draw radius.
- **Plain explosion (`Explsn`, mesh `hedra01`, flash `exflash`, scar `exscar`) and debris bricks (`2brk01`, `2brk02`, `4brk`, `6brk`)**: the sampled frames of the cases do not show them; unverified.
- **Cannon and rocket trails (`canstrk`, `mslstrk`)** are skid-trail decals (another branch).
- Shield and flame alpha in the starting and ending states (`0x0045d150`, `0x0045c2a0`: the device's alpha override, slot `0xb8`), the turbo's wheels (`ffweel`) and the car's shrink in the warp's last 250 ms (the fade's figure scale does not scale the drawn parts).
