# Power-up visuals: what the original draws, against the port

`pnpm run render-trace` has sixteen power-up cases (`Royal Knights, power-up <kind> <brick>/<level>`: cannon `1`, shield `2`, turbo `3` and oil `4`, each at levels 0 to 3; RACEC0R0; the player is granted the brick at frame 300 and presses use at 305; frames 307 to 385). Run one with `pnpm --filter @lego-games/lego-racers-web exec vitest run --config vitest.trace.config.ts render-trace -t "power-up shield 2/0"` (`RENDER_TRACE_FRAMES=312` records one frame, `RENDER_TRACE_GROUPS=<file>` writes the mismatch groups).

The library the models come from is `GAMEDATA\COMMON\POWERUP.WDB`: its mesh and skeleton instances are what the exe looks up by name (`FUN_004119a0`): `Explsn` (mesh `hedra01`), `barrel`, `spikexp`, `warpprt` (mesh `warphole`), `magnet`, `TurboL0..2`, `turb0f1..2f2`, `dmissil`, `shield0..3`, `shldin0..3`, `curse`, `cgreen`, `magring`, `insd`, bricks. Keyword `0x3f` of an instance is the texture scroll per second (`warpprt` 3, 2; `shield0..3` and the first flame 0, 4; the second flame 0, 2). Textures and materials come from the libraries `DTURBO0..2`, `POWERUP`, `GRAPPLE`, `WARPHOLE`, `CURSE`, `BARREL`, `CGREEN`.

Verified: `golden-trace` scenario `royal-knights-curse` (the computers use curses from frame 164, the player drives into one at 191, 580 frames, six racers) matches bit for bit in the new fields `racer.curseOrbit` (`+0x378`, `+0x37c`), `racer.curseFigure` (the figure's rotation, position and scale from `+0x2c4`) and `racer.override` (`+0x3c4`, `+0x3c8`), as well as in everything the trace compared before, also with every racer handed the curse. The render-trace case `Royal Knights, curse hit` fails without the curse figures (327 triangles of `curse1`, reported as `unmatched ee51a0cde6ae2c1d`, missing in frames 186 to 290) and has none missing with them.

Texture scroll (all the scrolling figures: shields, turbo flames, warp, curse rings): `GolDP.dll 0x10027f40` adds `milliseconds * rate` to a 16-bit offset (`+0x60`, `+0x62`), the rate being the instance's keyword `0x3f` as an integer: `trunc(rate * 65.536)` units of 1/65536 a millisecond. A scroll of 1 a second therefore turns 0.8 percent slower (65 of 65.536). The viewer's `setTextureScroll` does this now; the curse rings' texture coordinates match the original to 0.001 over 300 frames (before, they drifted 0.00027 a frame).

Hypotheses and open points of the curse:

- **Draw cut.** The original leaves a curse figure out beyond 100 units of the eye (the render trace: a figure at 89.6 is drawn, one at 101.7 is not; the bricks' level distance is 400) and when its centre is about 3 or more units outside a plane of the camera's frustum (centres at -1.3 are drawn, at -3.7 and beyond are not). Both are **hypotheses** fitted to the trace (`CURSE_LEVEL_DISTANCE`, `CURSE_BOUNDING_RADIUS` in `race-effects.ts`); the engine's figure draw (`0x0040e270`) tests the figure's own sphere, not read here.
- **Racer 4's figure at frames 195 and 200** of the case (about 47 units from the eye) is drawn by the original with fewer triangles than the port's (4 of 33 at frame 195) and 19 `curse1` triangles are missing at depth 43; its frame equals the original's (golden trace), so the difference is in the pose of the clip (a wrap, or parts the engine culls one by one). Not explained.
- **Tint lost.** In the case, racer 4 (code `CC`, cursed since frame 169) shows its car untinted from frame 260 on, while its override flag (`+0x3c4`) stays set; the port keeps the tint (`CCCM/all` diffuse 67,59,0 in the original against 11,87,11). The car's colours at that moment are the track's dark zone (halved), so a track event seems to reach the models in spite of the override; `0x004400a0` as read does not do that.
- A curse that missed the ground (no plane written, `0x00456360` leaves it uninitialised) draws nothing here. The curse figure is not drawn in the warp's own draw (`0x0045d940`); neither is a hypothesis traced.
- The curse's sound (`RaceSounds_Get` 9 at the hazard, 8 at the cursed racer, following the racer figure) is not played.

## Not ported (open)

- **Debris bricks (`brick1..4`, `0x004514b0`, `0x004515d0`)**: not drawn. The piece's figure takes the model, the attachment slot, a position (stack argument 2) and a first axis (argument 3, third axis up) and clip `thrownCount % clipCount`; no sampled frame shows a piece (frames 492 to 520 of `Royal Knights, debris` have pieces far from the camera), so there is nothing to verify against.
- **Effect emitters**: the cannon's and grapple's `cannsmk` follow the owner (above); `dynsprk`, `lghthit`, `oilbub`, `trbsmke` (`0x0045ce20`) are still never positioned.
- **Spiked explosion**: drawn (`spikexp`, scale and opacity as the plain one) and equal to the original's triangles on `Royal Knights, barrel explosion` (frames 388 to 430). Its flash (`exflash`) is not created by `0x00421250` for these pools and its scar (`exscar`) is a decal (another branch).
- **Cannon and rocket trails (`canstrk`, `mslstrk`)** are skid-trail decals (another branch).
- The shield's starting and ending alpha (`0x0045c2a0`) is not ported; the turbo's level-2 ending frame 355 still differs by about 3 pixels (the car turns).
