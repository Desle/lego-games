# Camera trace: every race camera, original against port, per frame

[camera.md](camera.md) describes the camera code; this page is the check that it matches. The golden trace's machinery ([golden-trace.md](golden-trace.md)) runs the real race in the emulator and the port's `stepRace` + `stepRacePresentation` beside it with the same frame times and keys, and compares what the camera shows after every frame, bit for bit.

```
pnpm --filter @lego-games/lego-racers exec vitest run --config vitest.golden-trace.config.ts camera-trace
CAMERA_TRACE_RACES=RACEC1R0 CAMERA_TRACE_MIRRORING=forward CAMERA_TRACE_MODES=player ... # one race, one pass, one mode
CAMERA_TRACE_REPORT=<file> CAMERA_TRACE_DUMP_FRAMES=60,61 ...                             # report and the raw words of some frames
```

Both specs skip without the virtualenv or the original files and are not part of `pnpm run check`. The default run is the 26 races (13 forward, 13 mirrored; `LEGORACE.RCB` lists no mirrored final race, but the settings flag mirrors it all the same) in two modes, about 20 minutes in one process; the finish camera runs a whole race (RACEC1R0 in the default run; `CAMERA_TRACE_MODES=finish` runs any).

## What is compared

`golden_trace.py` with `isCameraOnly` (the scenario skips the racer snapshot) reads, after every frame, for the first local player (a demonstration has one: racer 0):

| Field                      | Original                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Port                                               |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------- |
| `camera0.mode`             | camera `+0x02`, `+0x04` (mode, look behind)                                                                                                                                                                                                            | `CameraState.mode`, `isLookingBehind`              |
| `camera0.basis`, `.eye`    | the eased basis `+0x48` and eye `+0x6c`                                                                                                                                                                                                                | `basis`, `position`                                |
| `camera0.target`           | target basis `+0x08` and eye `+0x2c`                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | `targetBasis`, `targetPosition`                    |
| `camera0.chase`            | heading `+0xd8`, look-at point `+0xe4`, steering angle `+0xf4`, full-throttle flag `+0x11c`, factor `+0x120`                                                                                                                                           | the same members                                   |
| `camera0.preset`           | `+0x10c..+0x118`, finish offsets `+0x124..+0x130`, ease rates `+0xf8`, `+0xfc`, mode clock `+0xc8`, step `+0xcc`                                                                                                                                       | the same members                                   |
| `camera0.node.position`    | the camera node's position, `+0x40` of the object the frame's `+4` points to                                                                                                                                                                           | `CameraView.position`                              |
| `camera0.node.orientation` | the `(forward, up)` arguments of the node's last orientation call: a hook on `GolDP.dll` `0x10002a30` (the call is the only way orientation reaches the node; the hook must exist before the code first runs because Unicorn caches translated blocks) | `CameraView.forward`, `.up`                        |
| `camera0.view`             | the frame's field of view `+8`, aspect `+0xc`, near plane `+0x10`, far plane `+0x14`                                                                                                                                                                   | `fieldOfViewDegrees` and the constants 4/3, 5, 800 |
| `camera0.racer`            | the racer's display rotation and position, forward speed, turn radius, body and driver flags, turbo level                                                                                                                                              | the same members of the port's racer               |

`camera0.racer` is not judged; it is what the camera reads. Where the port's racer differs from the original's (a physics divergence, see the golden trace), the camera is judged only up to that frame.

## Modes

| Mode            | Script                                                                                                                          | Cameras it reaches                                                                                      |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `demonstration` | `localPlayerCount: 0` (the attract mode: computers drive, the camera follows racer 0), 260 frames                               | the introduction fly-by (the clip, then the 2,000 ms ease), the countdown snap, the behind-car preset 0 |
| `player`        | idle through the countdown, throttle, four taps of the camera binding (rows 1, 2, the cockpit, row 0), 40 frames looking behind | every behind-car distance (rows 0 to 2), the cockpit, the rear view                                     |
| `finish`        | a whole demonstration race                                                                                                      | the finish camera (row 4, mode 3) and what follows                                                      |

There is no camera code of its own for the ghost, the replay or the attract mode: `Camera_Update`'s callers are `Race_LoadTrackContent`, `Race_Update`, the pause menu's restart (`0x004362e0`, the same load sequence) and the warp's draw (`0x0045d940`), so a ghost race, a replay or the demonstration drive the same camera objects (the session replay re-runs the race with recorded controls). The field of view is 65 and the near and far planes 5 and 800 in every race (the trace reads them from the frame each frame; the warp and the curse change the field of view, see [pixel-trace.md](pixel-trace.md) finding 4).

## Open

- Two scenarios (`racec0r0-mirrored-demonstration`, `-player`): the introduction clip's node orientation is one unit in the last place off in places while the position matches. The original mirrors the animation keys at load (`0x004015e0` negates the y of translation keys and the y and w of rotation keys, `0x0040ea20`) and the port flips y of the finished vectors; **hypothesis:** an operation order in the key interpolation or the bone matrix differs for mirrored keys. The other 11 mirrored races match bit for bit.
- The racer divergences the camera trace runs into (`camera0.racer[12]` onward, forward speed one to 13 units in the last place from frame 270 to 590, a body flag `0x80000` the port lacks on RACEC2R2 at frame 511) are the physics family of golden-trace divergence 8, not camera code; the camera is judged before them.
- The viewer's three.js camera uses the near plane 0.5 and the far plane 20,000 for drawing, where the original's frame holds 5 and 800 (the frustum used for culling has the right ones, `camera-frustum.ts`). That is the draw distance, left to its own work.
