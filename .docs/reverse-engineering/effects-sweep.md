# Effects sweep: particles, emitters, trails and animated effects against the original

[render-coverage.md](render-coverage.md) lists what the original draws that the port has no partner for. This page is the **class sweep of the effects** (particle systems, emitters, ribbons and trails, animated effect figures) on all 26 races: the mismatches grouped by mechanism, each mechanism read from the original's code, the fixes and what is still open. The proofs are `effects.trace.spec.ts` (`pnpm --filter @lego-games/lego-racers-web exec vitest run --config vitest.trace.config.ts effects.trace`), the pixel case `Royal Knights, power slide` and the unit specs named below.

## The measure

Triangles of effect textures and effect figures (`tflame*`, `smoke*`, `csmoke`, `watmist*`, `snowflak`, `mslstrk`, `canstrk`, `litflash`, `lightng`, `bubble*`, `rope`, `welder*`, `smturbo*`, `aftrburn`, `DT*BURN`, `DT*FLA*`, `SHEOUT*`, `DMISSILE`, `/tether`, `/spsmoke*`, `/tbsmoke*`, `/dksmoke*`) over the 701 trusted frames of `pnpm run render-coverage`: original triangles with no partner (**missing**), port triangles with no partner (**extra**) and shared triangles that differ in texture, UV or colour (**mismatch**; the alpha-test state is left out, it is the colour key stand-in). Computer racers diverge from the original after about frame 211 (power-ups), so every number here still contains pairings of triangles of cars that are not in the same place; the table below is the ranking, not a coverage.

## What the sweep showed that is not a mechanism of its own

- **Clipped triangles.** Many unmatched flame, smoke and fan triangles lie on the viewport's edge (y 480): the original's driver clips them, the port draws whole triangles. `effects.trace.spec.ts` leaves triangles on the edge out.
- **Pairings of cars that are not in the same place.** The `mismatch` groups of `DT*BURN/*`, `SHEOUT*` with another texture ("texture adnubint -> shetest4", "colorOperation select_diffuse -> modulate") pair a port figure with an unrelated original triangle near it: the computer's turbo or shield is on another frame. Not an effect gap.

## Open (found, not done)

- **Cannon and missile trails (`canstrk`, `mslstrk`, `aftrburn` streak).** A trail class the port does not have: 0x2a4-byte objects in the pool at world `+0x44` (`Trails_Take` `0x004939b0`, `Trail_Start` `0x00492ae0`: duration 300 ms, ring size 4, 4 vertices a ring, colour `0x004c1c4c`, material `canstrk` through `0x00492a90`), fed every update by the cannon (`0x00451b50`, tail) and the rocket (`0x004568f0`) with a ring of four points round the ball: with `w` = the horizontal unit perpendicular of the ball's velocity (`(vy, -vx)`) times 3, `p0 = (b - w/2, bz + 1.5)`, `p1 = (b - w/2, bz - 1.5)`, `p2 = (b + w/2, bz - 1.5)`, `p3 = (b + w/2, bz + 1.5)`, skipped when the velocity has no horizontal part (`0x00492ee0`: the ring and the ball's position); `Trail_Advance` `0x00492c30` ages the ring buffer, `Trail_BuildMesh` `0x004931a0` writes the vertices (ring interpolation by the fade factor `+0xd0`), indices and colours into a dynamic mesh. Updated by `Trails_Update` `0x00493a20` from `Race_Update`. The sweep counts 440 triangles (`mslstrk` 180 missing, `canstrk` 39, `aftrburn` 18, extras).
- **Lightning (`lightng`, `litflash`).** Computer wands in `att2` and `rkr` (`lghthit` sparks: `0x00455440` places `lghthit` on the struck racer every update, `0x00454d70`); the lightning ribbon is ported, the strike frames of computers diverge. 304 triangles.
- **Positions of `dynsprk` (barrel sparks, `0x00453240` state 2) and `lghthit`.** Started by the effects but not positioned (they sit at the origin; the census found 223 and 122 frames).
- **Snow in the introduction.** From frame 60 to 110 of a race the camera object the hook reads (`race + 0x2acc`, `+4`) is not the displayed camera: Ice Planet frame 100 reads position (-639.8, 729.2, -129.2) against the displayed (-651.9, 625.8, -146.1); both agree from frame 130. The snow of the introduction (about 14 original flakes unmatched) needs that camera. **Hypothesis**: the object holds the introduction path's own state while the lock puts the displayed camera on the bone.
- **A released snow emitter.** After the colour shift starts the original draws one of the 15 flakes alive (`Emitters_Draw` leaves the rest out; the port draws all). The draw conditions of a system flagged dying (`0x10`) are not traced.
- **Wheel and surface emitters of computers** (`grassemt`, `sandemt`, `wateremt`...): the port starts none for computers; no sweep frame shows a gap.
- **Debris, welder (`mmm`), `watmist4` (`ama`)**: the track pools' emitters named by events; positions and starts per class are not compared.
- **Flame phase at the start boost** (frame 170 of `rr`): the flame's alpha differs by 6 (111 against 117, 11 ms of timer) and the clip frame by one; the ending-state timer after the overshoot of the starting state is a candidate.
